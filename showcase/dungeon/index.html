A kinetic noir documentary · in four chambers

DUNGEON
OF AGREEMENTS.

Every relationship is an architecture of small promises. This is a walk through one after the load-bearing walls were quietly removed — and a record of what it takes to stand upright again.

Descend
I

Chamber I · The Vessel

A body keeps
the minutes.

Long before the mind admits what is happening, the body has already filed its report. The clay figure beside you is rebuilt from that report — watch it as you descend. It holds together the way a person holds together: by agreement, tension, and trust distributed across a thousand small points. Remove enough points, and shape becomes debris without a single dramatic blow ever being struck.

Chamber II · The Double Ledger

What was written
/ what was lived.

On the left: the agreements, as stated — calm, reasonable, notarized by repetition. On the right: the footage that plays underneath them. Scroll, and let the two ledgers disagree in front of you. The double-bind lives in the gap.

“We tell each other everything. Radical honesty, always.” stated at the kitchen table · winter
“This is a safe place. You never have to perform here.” stated during the first hard month
“You're free. No one is keeping you here.” stated whenever leaving was mentioned

III

Chamber III · The Co-Regulator

Smooth the signal.

This is a dysregulated nervous system, rendered honestly: jagged, fast, allergic to rest. Drag the somatic stones onto the water. Where a stone lands, the signal remembers how to be slow. This is not a metaphor for fixing someone else. It is a rehearsal for regulating yourself.

Three stones: breath, ground, witness. Place them where the water is worst. Drag them off the canvas to take them back.

Chamber IV · Daylight

The dungeon was
never locked.

Agreements can be rebuilt — but only with parties who believe words are load-bearing. The figure reassembles. Differently. It keeps the crack-lines visible on purpose.
